
# Guidelines

Laws should link to the original source if at all possible, rather than a Wikipedia article about them, an article discussing them, etc.

When discussing a law in the body section, do not restate it or reiterate the year. That is needlessly redundant.

Do nut use dangling modifiers (e.g. "this" that is not followed by a noun.) For precision, "this" and similar modifiers should be followed by a noun.
