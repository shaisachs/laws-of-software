---
title: Programming as Theory Building
author: Peter Naur
link: https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/Naur.pdf
year: 1985
---

Naur argues that programming is not merely the production of a program, but the building of a theory of how that program and its world behave. Because this theory lives in the minds of the programmers, it cannot be recovered from the program text alone; losing the people who hold the theory causes the program to become unmaintainable, which casts doubt on the value of purely formal software development methods.
