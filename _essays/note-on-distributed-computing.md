---
title: A Note on Distributed Computing
author: Jim Waldo, Geoff Wyant, Ann Wollrath, Sam Kendall
link: https://scholar.harvard.edu/files/waldo/files/waldo-94.pdf
year: 1994
description: This position paper argues that distributed computing differs from local computing in kind, not just in degree. Latency, memory access, concurrency, partial failure, and access are fundamentally different in a distributed system, so modeling remote interactions as ordinary procedure calls hides exactly the problems that most often bite. The authors recommend designing distributed systems explicitly around those differences instead of pretending they do not exist.
