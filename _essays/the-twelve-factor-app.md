---
title: The Twelve-Factor App
author: Adam Wiggins
link: https://12factor.net/
year: 2011
---

A methodology for building software-as-a-service applications that aims to minimize the time it takes to onboard new developers, maximize portability between execution environments, and enable continuous deployment. The twelve factors - from a single codebase and explicit dependency declaration, through configuration via environment variables, stateless processes, and treatings logs as event streams - capture modern best practices for building applications that are declarative, portable, and easy to scale.
